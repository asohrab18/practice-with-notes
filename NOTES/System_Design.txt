Q.[1] What is Scalability in System Design?
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TXJP0gzCXFQ

Scalability:
=========================
Ability of system to handle more users/load.
Should not slow down when traffic increases.


Two types:
==========
Vertical scaling (increase server power)
Horizontal scaling (add more servers)
Used to avoid system crash
Important for large applications

Q.[3]: What is Load Balancer simple explanation?
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCR3SAVdyCc

Load Balancer is used to distribute the users requests (Load) among our multiple servers to handle it. It enhances the performance of our application. 
It distributes the traffic in round robin fashion or randomly on the basis of some algorithm.
