Brute force means:
Try all possible options and pick the correct one.

* Real-Life Analogy:
Imagine, You lost your keys in your house.

Brute Force Way:
Check every room
Check every drawer
Check every corner

Eventually you’ll find it, but it’s slow.

* Smart Way:
Think: “Where did I last use it?”
Check only that place
That’s optimization (like HashMap)

Brute Force vs Optimal:
========================================================
Type	    | Meaning                      |   Speed
========================================================
Brute Force	| Try everything               |	Slow 
Optimal	    | Smart logic / data structure |	Fast 
========================================================

Why Brute Force is IMPORTANT
Don’t think it’s bad. It’s actually Step 1 of problem solving

Interview flow:
Solve using brute force ✅
Then optimize ✅
